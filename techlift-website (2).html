Why It Matters

Closing the Digital Divide

Millions of students across the United States lack access to quality computer science education. The digital divide isn't just about internet access - it's about opportunity, exposure, and the knowledge to compete in a technology-driven world.

TechLift exists to change that. By partnering with libraries, community centers, and schools in underserved areas, we bring free, high-quality coding education directly to students who need it most - at no cost to them or their families.

When students learn to code, they don't just learn a skill. They learn to think critically, solve problems creatively, and believe in their own ability to build something new. That confidence changes everything.

Our Story

About TechLift

A student-led nonprofit on a mission to expand access to computer science education in underserved communities.

Our Mission

Built by Students. Built for Students.

TechLift is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to expanding access to computer science education and technology opportunities in underserved communities. Through partnerships with libraries, community centers, and schools, we provide free coding classes, workshops, hackathons, and other STEM programs that help students develop valuable technical and problem-solving skills.

Our flagship program offers free JavaScript coding classes through the Code.org curriculum, taught by trained student instructors. As we grow, we aim to expand into additional areas of technology education and create more opportunities for students to explore STEM careers.

TechLift also provides meaningful volunteer and leadership opportunities for students. Volunteers can earn community service hours while gaining real-world experience in teaching, curriculum development, marketing, event planning, fundraising, outreach, nonprofit management, and leadership.

By empowering students as both learners and leaders, TechLift works to bridge the digital divide and create a future where every student has access to the technology education and opportunities they deserve.

Expectations

What We Expect From Council Members

All TechLift Council members are expected to contribute actively to the organization and help advance TechLift's mission. This is a real leadership commitment, not a title.

⏱️

Council members should expect to contribute approximately 3-6 hours per week depending on their role and the current phase of TechLift's programs and events.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I join with no prior coding experience?
Coding experience is recommended, since most TechLift members are expected to be able to teach or assist in teaching coding classes. However, prior experience is not strictly required - all volunteers and leaders are provided with thorough training, structured lesson materials, and clear instructions on what to teach. Depending on the position you apply for, more coding experience may be required, especially for instructor or education-focused roles. But there are also roles where beginners can learn and grow while contributing meaningfully to the organization.
How many hours per week is the board commitment?
Council members should expect to contribute approximately 3-6 hours per week depending on their role. During active program cycles or event seasons, the time commitment may be higher. All council members are encouraged to manage their time effectively and communicate with their team.
Do all board members have to teach?
Yes. All TechLift Council members are required to teach or assist in teaching coding classes at least once per program cycle. This requirement ensures that every council member stays connected to TechLift's core mission and understands the student experience firsthand.
What do I gain from being on the board?
TechLift Council members gain real-world experience in leadership, nonprofit management, teaching, public speaking, event planning, fundraising, marketing, community outreach, team management, and project management. This experience strengthens college applications, resumes, scholarship essays, and future career prospects.
